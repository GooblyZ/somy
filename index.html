Usage tips

  • Left click an item to select and drag it.
  • Drag on empty space to pan the view.
  • Mouse wheel zooms at cursor.
  • Draw mode lets you create items by dragging a rectangle.
  • Items can not overlap other items.
  • Merge joins two touching items to one L shape with two blocks.
  • Undo and Redo affect add, delete, move, rotate and merge.

Quick walkthrough

  1. Use the New furniture panel to add items numerically or draw with the mouse.
  2. Drag items to position; they snap to the grid. Toggle reality/design mode to allow overlaps.
  3. Use Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). Use the shortcuts panel for keys.
  4. Use the Units selector to switch between cm and inches/feet display.
