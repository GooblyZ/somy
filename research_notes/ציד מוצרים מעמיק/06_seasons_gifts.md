## 1. What sells abroad in this season/gift space, and what fits Israel?

### Takeaway
The strongest proven winter items are small, cheap fixes for a cold house, not big heaters. Door and window draft sealing went viral on TikTok US in winter 2025/26. Rechargeable hand warmers, moisture absorbers, wearable blankets and USB foot warmers all have solid Amazon volume even off-season. Israel's 2025 winter showed the same demand pattern: Super-Pharm reported heating products up 101% and winter blankets up 172%. In gifts, the evidence is strongest for travel jewelry cases (80K+ sold on AliExpress), the candle-warmer lamp (30K/mo on Amazon, +100%), baby print kits, mug warmers and projection necklaces.

### Inferences
- The best fit for Israel this winter is "cheap fixes for a cold, leaky, damp apartment": sealing tape, door draft stopper and moisture boxes. They're light and cheap to ship, easy to demo (the lighter-flame test), have proven US virality, and Israel's aluminium sliding windows and lack of central heating make the pain real. They sell in waves with each storm, so stock before the first big rain system and re-run ads on storm days.
- Personal warmth (hand warmers, heated vest, foot warmer, lunch box) has an Israeli-specific audience: reservists, guards, delivery riders and drivers. But every one of these is a battery or 220V product, so Israeli standards approval is the main gate.
- In gifts, the cleanest volume signal is the jewelry travel case (80K+ sold). The most "early" opportunity is the candle-warmer lamp: Amazon units doubled, but AliExpress sales are still low and I found no Israeli ads.
- Blanket hoodies and projection necklaces are proven but already have active Israeli advertisers. Only enter with a sharper angle (personalization, a bundle, or a price edge).

## Top 5 for Israel and why
1. **Window/door sealing tape plus a door draft stopper bundle (#4 + #3).** It has the strongest recent viral proof: 46K units on TikTok US in about 50 days, and 10K/mo on Amazon even in July. It solves a real Israeli problem (aluminium windows, no central heating), is light and cheap, needs no electrical certification, has a 5-second lighter-flame demo, and I found no visible Israeli advertisers (unverified). Sell it as a "winter seal kit" for about ₪49–79.
2. **Moisture absorber boxes (#5).** Winter mold and musty closets are a common Israeli complaint. Amazon shows 20K/mo, buyers come back for more, and a "water collected after a week" video is compelling. Ship before the rainy season peaks (Dec–Feb).
3. **Rechargeable magnetic hand warmer 2-pack (#1).** Proven abroad at 5–8K/mo even off-season, and it fits Israeli audiences: reservists, guards, riders, and a dad or soldier gift for Hanukkah. The battery needs certification, so pick a supplier with documents.
4. **Candle-warmer lamp (#18).** The "rising and early" pick: #1 in its Amazon category at 30K/mo and doubling, while AliExpress sales are still low and there are no Israeli ads in sight. 5. **Handheld steam cleaner for Passover (#22).** It's 30K+ sold on AliExpress, has a dramatic before/after demo, and Passover cleaning is Israel's biggest "cleaning season". Prepare from February. It's a 2500W device, so it needs certification and careful supplier vetting.
