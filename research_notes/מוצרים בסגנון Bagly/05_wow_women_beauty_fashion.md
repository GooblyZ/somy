# "Wow" products for an Israeli store: women's beauty tools, fashion accessories, jewelry, self-care and teen trends (researched 2026-11-14)

**Labels:** FACT means seen directly today in tool output. REPORTED means a secondary source said it. ESTIMATE means my own assumption or calculation. UNVERIFIED means plausible but not confirmed.

**Exclusions honoured:** scented and pheromone creams, LED masks, lash clusters, press-on nails, whitening strips, heatless curlers, name necklaces and flight bags were all left out.

**Methods**
- **AliExpress.** I used headless Chromium with the IL/ILS cookie, `shipCountry=IL` and sorting by orders. Title, price, sold count, URL and image were extracted from the result cards.
  - Where a card showed a "New shoppers" price (usually ₪3.59 or a similar welcome deal), I used the regular, struck-through price instead.
  - Every image URL was checked and returns HTTP 200 (image/webp).
- **Meta Ad Library.** I opened the country=IL, active-ads, keyword_unordered URL with a fresh browser per query.
  - "0 / אין מודעות" results are **UNVERIFIED**. A previous round found that empty renders look the same as true zeros.
  - Counts are fuzzy keyword matches, so I list the relevant advertisers.
- **Local prices.** Shopify `/products.json` worked for glammie.co.il, neckly.co.il and ecouaer.com. israeltech.co.il prices came from the page JSON. I also used Zap search through Chromium.
- **Profit formula (ESTIMATE).** Profit = P/1.18 − 3.6%×P (fees) − 5%×(P/1.18) (returns) − landed − 86 (CPA).
  - Landed cost = AliExpress price × units, plus about 5 ₪ for shipping/packaging. Most listings show "Free shipping over ₪42".
  - No customs or VAT is included (assumed under the personal-import threshold, as in note 10). No certification cost is included.
- **Caveat on CPA.** At the median CPA of 86 ₪, almost every item here leaves only 10–30 ₪ per order at 199–249 ₪. This matches the window in note 10.
  - The economics work only with a bundle that lifts the order value, or with a CPA below the median.

## Q3. Economics, compliance and key risks

### Takeaway
At CPA 86, profit per order ranges from −12 to +31 ₪.
- **Best margins:** neck massager (+31.4, but heavy), nylon bag (+31.2, but not a "wow" product), blackhead vacuum (+25.2), charm kit (+24.6) and self-care kit (+24.6).
- **Electrical approval:** 11 of the 15 items are battery or heating devices, which in Israel likely need electrical-safety or standards-institute approval.
- **Toy standard:** the kids' camera also needs the toy standard.
- **No electrical certification needed:** only the charm kit, bag charms, phone strap, bag and claw clips. None of the 15 is a cosmetic, so no cosmetics notification is needed. Adding a serum or gel to a bundle would change that.

### Cited Findings
- ESTIMATE: arithmetic for each item is in the cards above and in the `profit_arithmetic` field of the JSON.
- REPORTED (note 10, not redone): workable window is about 199 ₪ or more, landed cost at or below 25–30% of price, median CPA 86 ₪ — [10_unit_economics_verification.md](../מחקר%20מוצרים%20לאיקומרס%20בישראל/10_unit_economics_verification.md)

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE: landed cost as a share of price:
  - charm kit 21%, blackhead vacuum 21%, straightener 25% and strap 26% are inside the bar;
  - neck pillow 30% and the self-care kit 32% are at the edge;
  - the label printer at 38% fails.
- ESTIMATE: a 249 ₪ two-item self-care or gift bundle is the most realistic way to stay positive at CPA 86.
