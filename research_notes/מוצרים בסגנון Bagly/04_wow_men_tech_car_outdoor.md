Method notes (read these before using the numbers):
- **AliExpress price** = the regular price for a returning shopper, worked out as the shown "new shoppers" price plus the "New shoppers save ₪X" amount on the result card. It is not the new-user promo. Prices are in ILS as AliExpress displays them for region=IL (FACT, 2026-11-14). These regular prices are often inflated by AliExpress. A CJ or 1688 agent price may be lower (UNVERIFIED).
- **Landed cost** = AliExpress price, plus 0 if the card showed "Free shipping", otherwise plus the KakoBuy ePacket rate from file 10 (100 g 27 ₪, 300 g 38 ₪, 500 g 49 ₪; battery line 300 g 46.6 ₪, 500 g 63.8 ₪). These are ESTIMATES and an upper bound. Domestic delivery inside Israel is not included.
- **Profit at CPA 86** = P/1.18 − 3.6%×P (payment fees) − 5%×P/1.18 (returns) − landed − 86.
- **Meta Ad Library limitation:** several queries came back with **no results**, including very generic terms like "מנגל" and "מכונת גילוח". The longer "מכונת גילוח לקרחת" returned ~150 ads. So a "no ads shown" result is **UNVERIFIED** and may be a loading or throttling failure. Treat only the positive counts as FACT. The "oldest active" date is the oldest among the ads that loaded (up to ~30), not across the whole library.

### Inferences
- **The CPA is the binding constraint here.** Items whose AliExpress regular price is under ~25 ₪ (bib, cable, towel, wristband, lighter) only work as bundle add-ons. A hero product needs a sale price of ≥249 ₪ with landed cost ≤ ~90 ₪, or a pair at 199 ₪ with landed cost ≤ ~50 ₪.
- **Best fits with the Bagly logic**, in order:
  1. Wireless CarPlay adapter: money saved versus replacing the head unit, a 10-second demo, and no gadget advertisers in Hebrew.
  2. Bald-head shaver + bib bundle: demand is proven by ~150 Hebrew ads, but the market is competitive.
  3. Vacuum MagSafe mount pair: a strong visual.
  4. Sand-free beach mat: a typically Israeli pain, but seasonal. Prepare it for April 2027.
- **Price undercutting by Israeli retailers** (Bug 159 ₪ label printer, Bug 199 ₪ Carlinkit, Payngo 129 ₪ inflator, Homecenter 89.90 ₪ mini massage gun) kills the "feels cheap versus what it saves" effect for those items. Customers will compare prices on Zap or KSP.
- **Seasonality:** today is 14 Nov 2026. Car umbrella, beach mat and cooling towel are spring items. Shaver, CarPlay, mount and label printer can launch now (holiday gifting season abroad; Hanukkah in Israel).
- **Certification flags:**
  - Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/RF transmitters (CarPlay adapter, lavalier mic, Bluetooth printer, Bluetooth thermometer) likely need Ministry of Communications approval for commercial import (UNVERIFIED).
  - Lithium-battery items (shaver, inflator, massage gun, printer, lighter, mic) need battery shipping lines.
  - Heating and electrical items (beard brush, shaver) may need electrical-safety standard approval (UNVERIFIED).

### Gaps
- TikTok view counts and Kickstarter signals were not collected (no reliable source in this session). Abroad evidence is mostly AliExpress sold counts, plus REPORTED press for the beard bib and car umbrella.
- Meta "no ads shown" results are unreliable: generic terms like "מנגל" and "מכונת גילוח" also returned nothing. Re-check these by hand in a logged-in browser: car umbrella, label printer, beard bib and brush, plasma lighter, beach mat.
- No Israeli store price was found for the head shaver (Mensch), beard products, vacuum mount, meat thermometer, plasma lighter, cooling towel or lavalier mic. Zap scraping returned no parsable prices.
- Weights are estimates; no listing weight was read. CJ/1688 sourcing prices were not checked, and they may make several negative items profitable.
- Whether each RF device needs Ministry of Communications approval, and whether an exemption applies, was not verified on gov.il (blocked in prior research).
